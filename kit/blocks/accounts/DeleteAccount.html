Delete your account?

Here is what happens. Read it before you decide.

  • You’re logged out everywhere now, and nobody can see your profile.
  • For 30 days, until 17 November 2026, you can log in and cancel. Nothing is lost yet.
  • After that, your notes, pictures and comments are deleted for good. They can’t be brought back.
  • Pages you shared with others are deleted too. Copies they made stay theirs.
  • We keep invoices for 7 years because the law requires it. Nothing else.
