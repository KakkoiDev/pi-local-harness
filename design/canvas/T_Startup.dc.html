Startup header

Five lines, every start: name and build, Web, Gate, Phone, how to begin. The first start adds one line of suggestions. “Loop check: every 10 seconds” moves into /status: it is not something to act on.

Phone off says why in one line and points to /guide phone; at 60 columns the reason moves into the guide. With phone on, the QR sits to the right of the lines at 100 columns and wider, below them otherwise.

Links are OSC 8 hyperlinks, underlined, so they click in terminals that allow it and still read as addresses when copied.
