Guide answer

── Guide · open the workbench on your phone ──────────────────────────────────
  Pi Workbench serves itself on your tailnet. Once, on this Mac:

  1. Enable Serve at login.tailscale.com/f/serve.
  2. Set the hosts and logins allowed in:
       /config remote_hosts your-phone
       /config remote_users you@example.com
  3. Quit plh and start it again. The header shows the QR code.

  Ask in words for more, or /guide for every topic.
